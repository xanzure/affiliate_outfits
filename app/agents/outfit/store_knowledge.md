# Outfit Curator Reference Guide
# Use this as a strong reference, not a rigid rulebook. Real style has personality.
# Sources: TikTok aesthetic communities, editorial fashion, stylist common knowledge.

---

# PART 1: CONCEPT VIBES & HOW TO STYLE THEM
# Many concepts have multiple valid directions. Pick one and commit — don't blend.

## Bad Bunny Concert / Reggaeton Night
Two totally valid directions — choose based on the outfit's position in the batch:

**Direction A — Streetwear/Hype:**
- Jorts (denim shorts) + oversized graphic tee knotted or tucked at waist + chunky sneakers (New Balance 550, Nike Dunk)
- Baggy cargo pants + fitted crop top or sports bra + Air Forces or Jordans
- Jersey dress + bucket hat + chunky gold jewelry
- TikTok rule: if jorts are wide/baggy, top must be fitted or knotted. Never baggy on both.

**Direction B — Tropical/Reggaeton Glam:**
- Colorful or printed mini skirt + matching or complementary crop top + platform sandals
- Fitted halter dress in bold color (red, orange, cobalt) + strappy heeled sandals
- Cut-out bodycon + hoop earrings + mini bag
- TikTok rule: go bold on color here. Neutral is a waste of this concept.

**What never works for this concept:** flowy blouses, formal trousers, anything that reads "work meeting." The energy should be festival or night-out.

---

## Dark Academia
- Core formula: blazer OR chunky cardigan + trousers or midi skirt + loafers or oxfords
- Layering is essential — turtleneck under blazer, button-down under sweater vest
- Colors: deep brown, forest green, burgundy, camel, cream, black, plaid
- Accessories: leather bag, round glasses, plaid scarf, simple gold or silver jewelry
- TikTok says: the plaid blazer + dark trousers + loafers combo is the signature look. Works every time.
- Avoid: bright colors, sneakers (unless chunky vintage ones), anything too sleek or modern
- Shoe rule: loafers or oxfords always. Heeled boots for evening version. Never athletic shoes.

---

## Old Money / Quiet Luxury / Stealth Wealth
- No visible logos — quality communicates itself
- Neutral palette only: camel, ivory, cream, white, navy, grey, chocolate brown
- Textures matter: cashmere, wool, linen, silk, leather (not faux)
- Fit: tailored but relaxed. Never tight. Never sloppy.
- TikTok term: "stealth wealth" — looks expensive without announcing it
- Formula: wide-leg tailored trousers + fitted ribbed knit tuck + loafers or simple flats + minimal gold jewelry
- Bag: structured leather (A.P.C., Polene, or actual luxury)
- Avoid: anything trendy, loud, or fast-fashion looking. Timeless only.

---

## Y2K / 2000s Revival
- Low-rise bottom (low-rise jeans, mini skirt, denim mini) + crop top or baby tee
- Platform shoes or chunky sneakers (Buffalo London, chunky New Balance)
- Mini bag (Jacquemus Le Chiquito-style, fluffy, or logo-print)
- TikTok rule: if bottom is low-rise, top MUST be cropped to show some midriff. High-waist tops kill this vibe.
- Colors: lots of pink, baby blue, white, denim-on-denim is huge
- Accessories: butterfly clips, nameplate necklace, tiny sunglasses, hoop earrings
- Can also go: velour tracksuit (Juicy Couture energy), mesh top layered over bra

---

## Coastal Grandmother
- Linen, linen, linen — this is the fabric of this concept
- Oversized but deliberate — not sloppy, just relaxed
- Neutrals: sand, cream, white, sage, soft blue
- One statement piece: a bold striped top, interesting texture, or pop of color
- Shoes: espadrilles, loafers, simple leather sandals — never sneakers or heels
- Bag: woven straw tote or simple canvas tote
- TikTok reference: Diane Keaton, Ina Garten, Nancy Meyers movies
- Jewelry: simple gold, nothing chunky or statement

---

## Mob Wife / Maximalist Glamour
- Animal print is a building block, not an accent
- Fur coat or faux fur anything (bag, trim, full coat) — the key piece
- Chunky gold jewelry, layered, dramatic
- Pointed heels or knee-high boots
- TikTok: "rich widow energy" — over the top is the whole point
- Colors: black, cream, animal print, deep red, gold
- Rule: pick one maximalist statement piece and build around it. Everything else can be simple.

---

## Coquette / Bow Girl
- Bows — on the bag, in the hair, on the shoes, on the top
- Soft pink, cream, white, lavender, dusty rose
- Ballet flats (Repetto or Sam Edelman dupe) are the signature shoe
- Delicate gold jewelry — dainty chains, pearl earrings
- Mini or midi slip dress, wrap skirt, flowy feminine pieces
- TikTok: Lana Del Rey meets hyper-feminine. Sofia Coppola if she was on Pinterest.
- Never: chunky sneakers, anything sporty, oversized pieces

---

## Gorpcore / Outdoorsy-Chic
- Technical outdoor gear worn as everyday fashion — Arc'teryx, Patagonia, The North Face
- Trail runners: Salomon Speedcross or XT-6 are the TikTok signature shoe here
- Cargo pants or technical hiking pants worn casually
- Fleece vest or half-zip layered over simple tee
- TikTok: the point is you COULD go hiking but you're going to brunch
- Rule: one technical/outdoor piece anchors the look. Everything else can be more basic.

---

## BRAT / Club Kid / Rave-Adjacent
- Charli xcx's BRAT era: that specific green (#8ACE00), anti-glamour, raw
- Fitted tank or crop top + low-rise jeans or skirt
- Going-out direction: sparkly, metallic, cut-out, bodycon
- TikTok: intentionally low-effort but fashion-forward. Not trying too hard.
- Shoes: chunky platform boots or sneakers for BRAT; heeled sandals for rave/going-out

---

## Clean Girl / Minimal Aesthetic
- Slicked-back bun is the hairstyle but focus on clothes
- Neutral basics: white tee, beige trousers, fitted blazer
- Gold jewelry: small hoops, delicate chain necklace, minimal rings
- Shoes: white sneakers, loafers, or simple sandals
- TikTok: effortless, dewy, no-effort-but-lots-of-effort energy
- Formula: monochromatic neutral outfit + gold accessories + one elevated bag

---

## Euro Summer / Tomato Girl / Italian Vacation
- Sundresses, linen trousers, strappy sandals
- Colors: red (tomato red especially), terracotta, warm white, olive, cobalt
- Straw hat, woven bag, sunglasses
- TikTok: Roman Holiday energy, Amalfi Coast, Mediterranean market
- Rule: warm colors and natural materials only. Nothing synthetic or sporty.

## Signature Anchor Rule
When the concept is tied to a specific person, artist, or cultural event, at least ONE piece must
anchor the outfit to that specific identity. Generic streetwear alone is not enough.

- **Bad Bunny concert:** a pava (Puerto Rican straw hat), Bad Bunny merch tee, a PR flag piece,
  Adidas collab he's actually associated with, or something that reads distinctly Caribbean/Latino
  rather than just generic streetwear
- **Morgan Wallen concert:** a Wallen merch piece, cowboy hat, western boots, or something that
  reads distinctly country/Nashville — not just "casual American"
- **Artist/performer concepts generally:** official merch, a signature accessory associated with
  that artist's visual identity, or a piece that would make someone say "oh that's for [X]'s show"

For broad aesthetic concepts (Old Money, Dark Academia, Euro Summer, SoHo Summer, etc.) this rule
does NOT apply — those outfits should feel like the aesthetic itself, not any specific person.

## Gender Coherence Rule
- Every outfit must commit to either a masculine OR feminine direction. Do not mix signals.
- Feminine signals: hoops, delicate jewelry, mini skirts, heels, bows, crop tops styled as feminine, strappy bags
- Masculine signals: bucket hats, baggy jorts, chunky sneakers, jerseys, crossbody utility bags, boxy tees
- Mixing these (e.g. chunky sneakers + bucket hat + gold hoops) reads incoherent. Pick a lane.
- If the concept is ambiguous, default to feminine unless the brief clearly skews masculine.

## Cultural Authenticity Notes
- **Bad Bunny / Reggaeton:** Caribbean and Latino identity — think San Juan, not Tokyo. Brands that read
  more East Asian streetwear (Palace, some Supreme drops, certain Stussy aesthetics) can feel off.
  Better anchors: Nike, Adidas, New Balance, local Caribbean/Latino-adjacent brands, or neutral
  streetwear that doesn't have a strong cultural identity. Stussy works in a pinch but isn't the
  most authentic choice for this vibe.
- **Morgan Wallen / Country:** American South, Nashville, not coastal or urban. Brands: Wrangler,
  Ariat, Levi's, Justin boots, Carhartt (original, not WIP which reads more streetwear).
- **Dark Academia:** European/British intellectual tradition. Avoid anything too American-casual.

## Streetwear / Hype General
- Oversized tops with fitted or tapered bottoms, OR fitted top with wide/baggy bottoms — never both oversized
- Sneakers are the anchor piece — build the outfit around them
- Graphic tees should be worn slightly oversized and untucked (or half-tucked)
- Caps or beanies count as accessories here
- TikTok rule: one "hype" piece (special sneaker, collab tee) + simple everything else

# PART 2: UNIVERSAL PAIRING RULES
# These apply across all concepts. Reference when building any outfit.

## Volume Balance (the most important rule)
- Wide/oversized bottom → fitted or cropped top (always)
- Wide/oversized top → fitted bottom (tapered pants, mini skirt, slim jeans)
- Both oversized = intentional and fashion-forward ONLY if you're going for streetwear/oversized aesthetic
- TikTok calls this "the balance rule" — it's why some outfits look thought-out and others look like you grabbed random clothes

## Specific Bottom Pairing Rules
- **Jorts (denim shorts):** crop top, fitted tee, knotted oversized tee, corset top. NOT flowy blouses, blazers, or formal tops
- **Wide-leg trousers:** fitted knit, tucked blouse, simple tee tucked in. NOT oversized hoodie or boxy jacket (unless intentionally oversized look)
- **Mini skirt:** works with almost everything — oversized top (fashion), fitted top (classic), cropped jacket (put-together)
- **Maxi skirt:** needs a fitted or tucked-in top to avoid looking shapeless
- **Cargo pants:** crop top, tight tee, fitted jacket. Half-tuck works well here
- **Low-rise jeans:** cropped top only. Any high-waist or long top ruins the proportion
- **Slip skirt/dress:** can layer a fitted tee under (Y2K), wear alone, or put a fitted knit over

## Color Combinations TikTok Loves Right Now
- Brown + pink (especially dusty rose or hot pink)
- Burgundy + orange (unexpected but editorial)
- Cobalt blue + cream or white
- Olive/forest green + camel or tan
- All black (never wrong)
- Monochromatic in any color (same shade head to toe = always looks intentional)
- White + cream (tonal dressing)
- Navy + red + white (coastal/preppy but elevated)

## Shoe Pairing Guide
- **Loafers:** elevate everything. Academic, quiet luxury, clean girl, business casual. Very versatile.
- **Ballet flats:** coquette, feminine, soft. Never with sporty or streetwear pieces.
- **Chunky sneakers:** streetwear, Y2K, gorpcore. Add casual edge. Ground any oversized look.
- **Clean minimal sneakers (Veja, Common Projects):** quiet luxury, clean girl. More elevated than chunky.
- **Platform sandals:** Y2K, going out, summer festival, reggaeton concert vibe.
- **Strappy heeled sandals:** going out, evening, summer event, euro summer.
- **Cowboy boots:** boho, western, festival. Pair with mini skirts, flowy dresses, or straight jeans.
- **Heeled boots (ankle or knee-high):** add edge. Work with minis, midi skirts, wide-leg trousers.
- **Trail runners (Salomon):** gorpcore specifically. Looks wrong outside that context.

## Jewelry and Accessories
- Chunky gold jewelry → streetwear, reggaeton, mob wife, maximalist looks
- Delicate gold → clean girl, quiet luxury, coquette, euro summer
- Silver → dark academia, grunge-adjacent, Y2K sometimes
- Layered necklaces → works for most concepts, adds dimension
- Statement earrings → going out, coquette, euro summer
- Mini bag → Y2K, going out, coquette
- Tote/structured bag → quiet luxury, dark academia, clean girl
- Straw bag → coastal, euro summer, boho

## What Makes an Outfit Look Expensive (TikTok's "expensive formula")
1. Fit is correct — nothing is pulling, gaping, or dragging
2. Shoes match the vibe — wrong shoes ruin any outfit
3. Minimal accessories but right ones
4. Color palette is intentional — not random
5. One piece does the talking; others support it

## Price Guidelines
Keep outfits accessible. Target price points:
- budget: under $30 (SHEIN, Amazon, ASOS sale, H&M)
- mid: $30–$80 (ASOS, Zara, Mango, Steve Madden, Sam Edelman, Urban Outfitters)
- elevated: $80–$150 (Revolve, Nordstrom, Madewell, Levi's premium)
- luxury: $150+ (use sparingly, max one piece per outfit if at all)

Most outfits should sit in the budget-to-mid range. A full outfit should feel achievable under $200 total.
Amazon and SHEIN are valid sources for budget pieces — accessories, basics, jewelry especially.
Avoid suggesting pieces over $150 unless the concept explicitly calls for it (Old Money, luxury vibes).

## Search Query Tips
- Always include the specific store name when you know it's a strong match: "Adidas Samba sneakers", "COS wide-leg trousers"
- Be specific about color and material: "sage green linen wide-leg trousers" not "green pants"
- For luxury concepts include brand names in queries
- For sneakers, include the specific silhouette name (Samba, Air Force 1, etc.)
